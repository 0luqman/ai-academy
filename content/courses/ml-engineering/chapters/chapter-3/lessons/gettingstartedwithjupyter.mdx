Let’s walk through the process of working with a Jupyter Notebook.


There are multiple ways to open a Jupyter Notebook. If you have Anaconda, you can continue to launch from the Navigator like we did [when we installed Jupyter Notebook](/learn). Note that Jupyter Notebook works best on Chrome, Safari and Firefox browsers.


If you would prefer, here is [a video walkthrough](/learn).


If you have Miniconda or just want to use the Command Line, open the BASH/zsh Shell (Mac) or Anaconda Prompt (Windows) and launch a Jupyter Notebook with the command:


jupyter notebook

We love having you here, but we also know that to achieve your goals, you will need to be able to work on your own computer. We still think that AI Academy by RiWoT is the best place to learn, but we know that your own computer (or a shared one) is the best place to build a portfolio.


With that in mind, we want to be sure that you are familiar with the best and most popular tools. For Data Science and Python, that is Jupyter Notebook. Data Scientists love Jupyter Notebooks because they make it easy to run experiments, jot down some notes, and share results with others (you know, like a scientific notebook). 


But don’t just take our word for it, dive in and check it out for yourself!

Next, Catherine wants to take a look at the churn rate.


Churn rate is the percent of subscribers to a monthly service who have canceled. For example, in January, let’s say AI Academy by RiWoT had 1,000 learners. In February, 200 learners sign up, and 250 cancel.


The churn rate for February would be: 


cancellationstotal subscribers=2501000+200=20.8%\frac{cancellations}{total\ subscribers} = \frac{250}{1000 + 200} = 20.8\%
